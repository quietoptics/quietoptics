What makes them different

Apps in these categories almost always flatter the user. A manifestation app celebrates every win and quietly forgets the misses. A prediction app shows you your hits. The result is a record that can only ever agree with you.

These do the opposite. The target is hidden before you start, the impression is sealed before the reveal, the scoring runs against a fixed vocabulary rather than your memory, and the statistics are the real ones. An honest instrument has to be able to tell you nothing is happening, and these will.

What we don't claim

Worth stating plainly, because it tends to be the first question.

  • We don't claim remote viewing, precognition or manifestation work.
  • We don't claim these apps will improve your results.
  • We don't sell training, readings, courses or certification.
  • What we claim is narrower: if you keep a record this way, the number at the end will mean something, whichever way it goes.
